Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

生效日期 Effective：2026-10-19 · 所属 JiMo Explore（个人品牌频道）

1. 我们是谁 / Who we are

本隐私政策适用于“JiMo Shorts Uploader”——一个由个人创作者（JiMo Explore 频道主）自行开发并仅自用的短视频分发工具。它运行在创作者本人的电脑上，用于把创作者自己制作的原创视频发布到自己本人的社交媒体账号。
This Privacy Policy applies to "JiMo Shorts Uploader", a short-video distribution tool developed and used solely by an individual creator (the JiMo Explore channel owner). It runs on the creator's own computer and is used only to publish the creator's own original videos to the creator's own social media accounts.

2. 我们收集什么数据 / What data we collect

工具仅在为你本人发布内容时，通过各平台官方 API 获取或产生以下数据：
The tool only obtains or generates the following data, via official platform APIs, when publishing on your behalf:
  • 账号公开资料（如用户名、账号 ID）：仅用于确认“授权账号 = 发布账号”，且为只读。
    Account public profile (e.g., username, account ID): used only to verify that the authorized account is the publishing account; read-only.
  • 你主动上传的视频文件及其标题、描述：仅用于发布到你自己的账号。
    Videos you upload, plus their titles and descriptions: used only to publish to your own accounts.
  • 发布记录日志：发布时间、平台、帖子 ID 等，保存于你本机，用于去重与异常排查。
    Publishing logs: publish time, platform, post ID, etc., stored locally on your machine for de-duplication and troubleshooting.
  • OAuth 访问令牌：仅保存在你本机（macOS Keychain），用于代表你调用官方 API。
    OAuth access tokens: stored only on your local machine (macOS Keychain) to call official APIs on your behalf.

3. 我们不收集什么 / What we do NOT collect

本工具不采集任何观众/粉丝数据，不读取评论、私信、浏览记录；不向任何第三方出售、共享或传输上述任何数据；视频仅在创作者本机处理，不上传任何第三方云。
The tool does not collect any audience/follower data (no comments, DMs, or view history); it does not sell, share, or transfer any of the above data to any third party; videos are processed only on the creator's local machine and are never uploaded to any third-party cloud.

4. 数据存储与安全 / Data storage & security

所有数据均保存在创作者本机：视频与发布日志存于本地磁盘，OAuth 令牌存于本机钥匙串。我们不运营服务器、不存储用户数据于远端。创作者可随时撤销令牌并删除本地日志。
All data stays on the creator's local machine: videos and publish logs on local disk, OAuth tokens in the local Keychain. We operate no servers and store no user data remotely. The creator can revoke tokens and delete local logs at any time.

5. 数据删除与联系 / Deletion & contact

如需删除你的相关数据或撤回授权，请通过以下邮箱联系我们，我们将在收到请求后协助删除本机记录并指导撤销令牌：jizihebob@gmail.com。
To delete your related data or withdraw authorization, contact us at jizihebob@gmail.com; upon request we will assist in removing local records and revoking tokens.

6. 政策变更 / Changes

本政策可能随工具演进更新，更新后将在本页面发布新版本日期。
This policy may be updated as the tool evolves; the new effective date will be posted on this page.
